## How Size Standards Work

The SBA assigns a size standard to every NAICS code. A company must be **at or below**
the threshold to qualify as a "small business" for federal contracting purposes.

There are two types of thresholds:
- **Revenue-based**: Average annual receipts over the past 3 fiscal years (in millions)
- **Employee-based**: Average number of employees over the past 12 months (full + part-time)

> Note: Affiliates count. If your company is affiliated with another, their revenue/employees
> may be aggregated into your total. This is a common protest ground.

## Set-Aside Types Relevant to SDVOSBs

| Set-Aside | Full Name | Who Qualifies |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| SDVOSB | Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business | ≥51% owned/controlled by service-disabled vet; SBA-certified or VA-verified |
| VOSB | Veteran-Owned Small Business | ≥51% owned/controlled by veteran; primarily used for VA contracts |
| WOSB | Women-Owned Small Business | ≥51% owned/controlled by woman; used for WOSB set-asides |
| 8(a) | SBA 8(a) Business Development | Economically/socially disadvantaged; requires SBA enrollment |
| HUBZone | Historically Underutilized Business Zone | HQ in HUBZone; 35% employees in HUBZone |
| SB | Small Business (unrestricted set-aside) | Any firm qualifying as small under the applicable NAICS |

## SDVOSB Certification Note (Post-2023)

As of January 1, 2023, the SBA (not the VA) manages SDVOSB certification.
- Companies must apply through the **SBA's certification portal** (certify.sba.gov)
- VA contracts still require SDVOSB status but now use SBA certification
- Legacy VA-verified firms had until December 31, 2023 to complete SBA certification

Always confirm active certification status in SAM.gov before bidding.

## Common Mistakes in NAICS Selection

1. **Using a broad code when a narrow one applies** — affects size standard and competition pool
2. **Picking the wrong sector** — e.g., using a services code for a product procurement
3. **Not checking the incumbent's code** — the contracting officer may have a preferred code
4. **Ignoring affiliated revenue** — related entities can push you over the threshold
